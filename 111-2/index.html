1+1=1

Identity is possible because difference exists.

FormatEnsemble
Period2019 →
Created byIna Colizza & Antonello Apicella
ArchiveOriginal archive photography: Lucas Kao & Mario Gambardella

The project

1+1=1 treats identity not as a fixed shape but as a relationship between self, difference and multiplicity — the many facets that can exist inside one person and one collective.

The work became part of Matrafisc’s international development and touring repertoire, connecting individual identity with the larger structures to which people belong.
