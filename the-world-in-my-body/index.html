The project

The World in My Body explores a woman’s rediscovery of strength after giving birth: recovery, transformation, resilience and rebirth expressed through the body.

The 2025 programme listed Alice Taylor as the dancer.

Creative context

The World in My Body

This 2025 solo places bodily change at the centre of the choreography. Rather than treating strength as a return to a previous state, the work approaches recovery and resilience as the discovery of a transformed body. The 2025 programme listed Alice Taylor as the dancer.

Themes

  • motherhood
  • recovery
  • transformation
  • resilience
