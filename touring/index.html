Touring / programming

Contemporary dance touring
shaped by place and context.

Matrafisc Dance works with theatres, festivals, universities, cultural organisations and site-specific spaces. Projects can be programmed as standalone performances or developed alongside workshops, residencies and creative exchange.

Programming

The repertoire includes intimate duets, ensemble work, site-specific performance and collaborative projects. We can discuss the artistic and practical requirements of a specific project with programmers and presenting partners.

Residencies & development

Some projects can be supported through research periods, local engagement or creative development in partnership with a venue or institution. This allows the presentation to respond to place rather than simply arrive as a fixed package.

International work

Matrafisc has developed and presented work across the United Kingdom and internationally, including activity in Italy, the Netherlands, Mexico and the United States.

For presenters and partners

Conversations can begin around a specific production or around the needs of a venue, festival, university or cultural programme. Matrafisc can discuss artistic context, space, audience engagement and how a performance may connect with a residency or workshop.
