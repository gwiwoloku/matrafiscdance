The project

Soul’s Paths is a site-specific performance built around an inner journey through friendship, love, fraternal bonds and sexuality. Dancers, live musicians and audiences share the same environment as the work moves from room to room.

The project grew from an interest in life stories and the complexity of ordinary human experience. It was supported using public funding by Arts Council England.
