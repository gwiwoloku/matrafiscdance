Periodo Blu

Two shades of blue. Two relationships with time.

FormatDance theatre · duet
Period2017–18
Created byIna Colizza & Antonello Apicella
ArchiveOriginal archive photography: Rosa Sansone & Kay Ohio Cleveland

The project

Periodo Blu explores the human condition using blue as its starting point. One character looks toward possibility and the future; the other is anchored to the past and to a darker psychological landscape.

A mannequin head becomes a symbol of lost contact with reality while the duet moves between creation and destruction, stillness and motion, connection and withdrawal.
