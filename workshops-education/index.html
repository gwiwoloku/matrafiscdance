Workshops / education

Contemporary dance workshops
built around creative research.

Matrafisc Dance offers contemporary dance workshops, educational projects, residencies and creative development built around the company’s choreographic research.

For students & training programmes

Sessions can focus on contemporary technique, movement research, composition, performance presence and the relationship between personal experience and choreographic material.

For artists & companies

Research-led workshops can support professional development, collaborative process and the creation of new material. Formats can be adapted to the experience of participants and the aims of the host organisation.

For universities & cultural organisations

Matrafisc can combine performance, workshop activity and creative development within a wider educational or residency programme.

What sessions can explore

Workshop content can draw on contemporary technique, improvisation, composition, performance presence, movement research and the transformation of personal experience into choreographic material. The emphasis can shift according to participants and context.

Connected to performance

Education can sit alongside the company’s repertoire or be developed independently as a research-led encounter for students, artists, universities and cultural organisations.
