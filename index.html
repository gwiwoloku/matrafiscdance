Movement as a way
to tell the truth.

Founded in Manchester by Italian choreographers Ina Colizza and Antonello Apicella, Matrafisc grew from a shared desire to bring different training, cultures and artistic instincts into one choreographic language.

The company’s research reaches beyond dance aesthetics into psychology, memory, identity and the way experience changes the body. Work has travelled across the UK and internationally, including Italy, the Netherlands, Mexico and the United States.
