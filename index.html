The company

Different histories.
One choreographic language.

Matrafisc Dance was established in Manchester in 2015 through the artistic collaboration of Italian choreographers Ina Colizza and Antonello Apicella.

Their research moves beyond dance aesthetics into psychology, memory, identity, relationships and lived experience — searching for movement that carries emotional truth.
