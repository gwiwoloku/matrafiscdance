The company

Different histories.
One choreographic language.

M

Matrafisc was born in Manchester from the artistic collaboration of Italian choreographers Ina Colizza and Antonello Apicella. Their work combines different training, cultural references and creative instincts into a shared language.

The repertoire moves through psychology, identity, memory, relationships and social experience, shifting between theatrical stages, site-specific work and international collaboration.
