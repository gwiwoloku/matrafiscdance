The project

Monkey & Leopard developed through Matrafisc’s artistic collaboration with Jo Lau and marked the company’s first project aimed specifically at children.

The project was developed through an Arts Council England-funded research and development process, extending the company’s movement language into work for younger audiences.

Creative context

Monkey & Leopard

Developed in 2019 with Jo Lau, Monkey & Leopard marked Matrafisc’s first project aimed directly at children. An Arts Council England-funded research and development process gave the company space to adapt its movement language and creative methods for younger audiences.
